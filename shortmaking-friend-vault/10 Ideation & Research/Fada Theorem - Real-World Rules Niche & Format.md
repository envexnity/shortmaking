# Fada Theorem (`fada throem`) — Channel Niche & Format Rules

> **Proof Point:** Our *"humans having crazy survival rules"* video hit **15k views** — more than anything else on the channel ever did.

Instead of standard *"In this world..."* videos that invent a fictional world with one weird rule, **Fada Theorem** flips the format: we explain **real-world rules** (biology, physics, everyday human quirks) as if they belong in a bizarre video game world.

---

## The 3 Non-Negotiable Rules of Fada Theorem

1. **Must Be Real:** Every rule covered must be an actual, real-world phenomenon or human/Earth mechanic — never made-up sci-fi or fake lore.
2. **Must Be Obvious to Anyone (Even Little Kids):** No obscure trivia or niche science jargon. Anyone watching — including a little kid — should immediately recognize what real-world thing is being described (e.g., sleep, baby teeth falling out, yawning, brain freeze, getting dizzy, blinking, goosebumps).
3. **Frame It Like a Weird Video Game World:** Describe the everyday rule deadpan, as if it's an absurd mechanic from a strange video game (e.g., *"In this world..."*, *"starter gear"*, *"AFK mode"*, *"stun-locked"*, *"level 6"*).

---

## Hard Bans (Never Use)
- **NEVER use `"Luckily... / Unluckily..."`** in any script, beat sheet, or brainstorm.
- **NEVER use `"patch-note"` style** framing.
- **Do not treat "it's Earth" like a hidden plot twist at the end.** Because Rule 2 makes it obvious early, lean into the absurd description and escalation rather than pretending it's a secret.

---

## Starter Concept Examples
- **Sleep:** *"In this world, every 16 hours your character shuts down for 8 hours and plays random hallucinations—and if you skip it, your stats start glitching."*
- **Baby Teeth:** *"In this world, you spawn with starter teeth, and around level 6 they literally fall out of your face so the real ones can load in."*
- **Yawning:** *"In this world, if one player's battery gets low and they open their mouth, it wirelessly triggers the same animation on everyone nearby."*
