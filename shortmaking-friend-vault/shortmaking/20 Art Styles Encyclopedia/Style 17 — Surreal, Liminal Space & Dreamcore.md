# Style 17 — Surreal, Liminal Space & Dreamcore

**Visual Benchmark:** Jared Pike (*Poolrooms*), Kane Pixels (*Backrooms*), *Severance* corridors, Giorgio de Chirico surreal plazas, René Magritte — **uncanny emptiness where hyper-familiar architecture stretches slightly too far**.

---

## 1. Spatial Psychology & Architectural Rules
- **Uncomfortable Geometry:** Corridors slightly too long (`40 m+`), doorways opening into sheer pool drops or brick walls, staircases climbing into flat ceiling panels, endless repeating office partitions (`Array` modifier + tiny variation so it feels subtly artificial).
- **Zero Human Presence, Hints of Utility:** Exit signs, pool ladders, thermostat dials, recessed vents, damp floor tiles — strictly empty of people or personal clutter!
- **Poolrooms Sub-Style:** Curved arched doorways, sunk square pools with crystal-clear turquoise water (`IOR 1.333`, gentle ripple normal + subtle caustics), white `10 cm × 10 cm` square ceramic mosaic tiles everywhere (`MaterialCraft` crisp grout + glossy glaze `Roughness 0.08`).

## 2. MaterialCraft, TextureCraft & EffectCraft Recipe
- **MaterialCraft:** Seamless repeating office carpet (damp stain patches), acoustic drop-ceiling panels, yellowing 1990s floral/chevron wallpaper, glossy ceramic pool tiles with dark hairline grout.
- **Lighting:**
  - **Backrooms / Office:** Flat harsh greenish-white `4100K` recessed fluorescent troffers (slightly flickering emissive planes, one dead bulb per hallway!).
  - **Poolrooms / Dreamcore:** Soft diffused skylight pouring from invisible ceiling shafts over pale pastel clouds or endless calm horizon water.
- **EffectCraft Optical Degradation (Optional VHS / Found Footage Pass):** Mild 1998 camcorder softness, subtle barrel lens distortion, VHS chroma bleed/tracking jitter, gentle bloom around fluorescent tubes, and faint `60 Hz` fluorescent buzz + vast tiled reverb in **SoundCraft**!

## 3. Camera & Composition
- **Uncanny Eye-Level Symmetry:** One-point perspective centered slightly awkwardly (`24 mm–28 mm` wide lens, `1.6 m` handheld height) with deep corridor vanishing points.

## 4. Style Anti-Patterns
- Adding cliché jump-scare monsters (destroys quiet psychological unease!).
- Over-grunged post-apocalyptic decay (liminal spaces are eerily *maintained* and clean, just slightly damp and forgotten).
