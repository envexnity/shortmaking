## 1. The 10-Point Short-Form Operating System

| # | Pillar | Key Rule / Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Idea Funnel** | Combine **proactive weekly research** (Reddit, X, TikTok, YT) with **reactive capture** during normal scrolling. |
| **2** | **Idea Storage ("Next 5")** | Avoid over-engineered Notion dashboards; keep a simple Kanban/list and always rank your **Next 5 best ideas** at the top. |
| **3** | **Consistency** | Pick a sustainable cadence (e.g., **every 2–3 days** or `3×/wk`, never `1/day` if it causes burnout). Never miss two planned days in a row. |
| **4** | **Engaging Hooks (`0–3s`)** | Combine **spoken + visual + text** pattern interrupts; package the core value/intrigue into the first 3 seconds. |
| **5** | **Easy to Understand** | Aim for a **reading score of 4–5** (simple, jargon-free language). **Keep visual subjects at eye level across cuts** so the viewer's eye never hunts. |
| **6** | **Continuous Learning** | Dedicate weekly time to studying editing, storytelling, and retention craft—reps alone plateau without craft study. |
| **7** | **Observing Outlier Creators** | Maintain a spreadsheet/watchlist of top niche creators and audit their outliers every few weeks to adapt proven formats. |
| **8** | **Format Experimentation** | Test new locations, angles, and cross-niche storytelling structures to uncover repeatable winners. |
| **9** | **Repurposing Winners** | **Remake your own past viral hits** with the same hook/pacing, tighter cuts (zero fluff), and extra value mined from the original comment section. |
| **10** | **Monetization** | Build diversified rails early (brand deals, affiliates, digital products, UGC, community) aligned with audience trust. |

## 2. Actionable Takeaways for `"In This World"` Roblox Storytime
1. **Eye-Level Cut Continuity (Tip 5)**: Across rapid `1–3s` R6/R15 scene cuts, keep the protagonist's eyes/face anchored near the **same vertical screen band (`y ≈ 35–42%` from top)** so fast cuts feel effortless rather than disorienting.
2. **Remake Proven World Rules (Tip 9)**: When an *"In this world..."* premise hits `1M+` views, produce a tighter Part 2 or alternate-rule remix using top viewer comments as the new twist.
