## 1. The 5-Bucket Viral SFX Taxonomy
Despite being only `75 s` long, this reference hit `770K+` views because it distills the exact **5 audio categories** every high-retention short-form editor relies on:

| Bucket | Role in Short-Form Timeline | Usage Pattern |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Transitions / Whooshes ("Def Sounds")** | Glue between cuts, zoom-ins, and text pop-ins | Placed **2–4 frames before** the visual cut/zoom peak (`t_cut - 0.08s`) so the transient lands on Frame 0 of the new shot. |
| **2. Risers & Hits (Impacts / Bass Drops)** | Tension build into a reveal + sub-bass punctuation on twist | Riser swells `0.5–1.5s` underneath escalating dialogue, cutting cleanly into a `60–80 Hz` bass hit on the punchline word. |
| **3. Cartoon SFX** | Comedic exaggeration (*boings, pops, bonks, slips, blinks*) | Essential for R6/R15 Roblox reactions, wide-eye pops, slapstick ragdolls, and 2D-in-3D character acting. |
| **4. Meme Melodies & Trending Audio** | Cultural shorthand & instant comedic recognition | Short `0.5–2.0s` stingers (record scratch, *Wake Up Filthy* / viral stings) during absurd twists or fourth-wall breaks. |
| **5. Background Music Beds** | Continuous rhythmic momentum under speech | Mixed at **`-6 dB` gated RMS** below voiceover (ducking `~35%` under speech) to eliminate dead air without masking consonants. |

## 2. Actionable Takeaways for `"In This World"` Roblox Storytime
- **Map Every Visual Event to a Bucket**:
  - Zoom-in / Camera whip → Bucket 1 (`Whoosh`)
  - Rule reveal / Twist → Bucket 2 (`Riser + Bass Hit`)
  - Roblox avatar reaction / Oof / Pop → Bucket 3 (`Cartoon + Roblox Foley`)
  - Absurd punchline → Bucket 4 (`Meme Sting`)
  - Continuous bed → Bucket 5 (`Quirky/Suspenseful Loop`)
