## 1. The 3-Step Hook Formula (`0:35–9:32`)
Kallaway models a hook as stopping a viewer driving `70 mph` down a highway, making them exit, and turning them around via a **Curiosity Loop** in `3–4` sentences:

| Step | Name | Function & Mechanics | Example (*8M-view Vegas Sphere Short*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Context Lean** | Establish **topic clarity** immediately (so target viewers self-select in) + create a lean-in via common ground, benefit/pain point, metaphor, or mind-blowing scale. | *"The tech in the Vegas Sphere is insane. Biggest screen ever built, 20 times bigger than an IMAX."* |
| **2** | **Scroll-Stop Interjection** | A single "stun gun" line using a **contrasting pivot word** (`But`, `However`, `Yet`, `Although`, `Get this`) that halts forward expectation. | *"But get this: the screen is actually the least impressive part of the whole thing."* |
| **3** | **Contrarian Snapback** | The haymaker—stays on topic but snaps the viewer onto an unexpected opposite path. Bigger shock = bigger snap. | *"Because the most impressive part is the audio. This is going to blow your mind."* |

## 2. The 5 Hook Multipliers (`9:32–16:34`)

1. **Visual Hooks (`100×` more powerful than spoken words alone)**:
   - People **read faster than they hear** and faster than they parse a full scene.
   - **3–5 Word Big Bold Title Text**: Put `3–5` clear words on screen (e.g., *"Future of Home Design"* with arrows, not obscure jargon) to lock Context Lean instantly.
   - **Goldilocks Motion**: Enough movement in frame 0–2s to trigger the brain's peripheral-motion reflex (not static, not chaotic blur).
2. **Benefit / Pain-Point Framing (*"People Like Hearing This..."*)**:
   - Lead with the **desired outcome or pain**, not the technical mechanism (e.g., *"If you want better sleep, take magnesium"* beats *"Magnesium is 1 of 21 core minerals"*).
3. **Cult Hopping**:
   - Wrap unfamiliar or niche concepts inside a **known brand, celebrity, or cultural anchor** (e.g., Taylor Swift, IMAX, Apple) so viewers feel smart and comfortable rather than alienated.
4. **Compress Speed to Value (`<4s` Timer)**:
   - Short-form has a **~4-second fuse** before exponential drop-off. Frontload the first hit of value or intrigue inside or immediately after the hook; chain **Context → Immediate Value → Context → Immediate Value**.
5. **Staccato Sentences**:
   - Open with **ultra-short, punchy staccato sentences** to maximize value-density per word when attention is scarcest, then vary into medium/long sentences later for rhythm.
