## 2. The 25 Non-Negotiable Laws of Viral Animated Shorts

### A. Scripting & Pacing Laws
1. **Sentence 1 Is a Paradox Stated as Fact (`10–16 words`)**: Never open with *"In 1831..."*, *"Did you know..."*, or *"Today we're looking at..."*. Open with the absurdity or physical contradiction already in motion: *"In 1880, a British bridge collapsed because soldiers were too polite to march out of step."*
2. **Word Rate Is `3.5–4.1 words/sec` (`3.7 wps` median) for Toon/Realistic/Roblox, or `2.6 wps` for Low-Poly HUD Explainers — Strict `< 45s` Duration Cap**: All future videos MUST be **strictly under 45 seconds (`< 45s` hard cap; `28–42s` sweet spot, `105–155 words` across `8–12 beats`)**. Zero dead air: pauses `>0.25s` must total `<1.0s` across the entire video.
3. **BANNED — NEVER Use `"Luckily... / Unluckily..."` or `"Patch-Note"` Style**: Never write *"Luckily... / Unluckily..."* reversals or *"patch-note"* framing in any script, beat sheet, or brainstorm. Use natural escalation and fresh reversals instead.
4. **The `"You see,"` Analogy Pivot**: At ~55–65% through the script, pivot from narrative story to physical mechanism using the exact phrase `"You see,"` paired with a tactile everyday analogy (e.g., pushing a child on a swing at the exact top of the arc).
5. **Callback Twist on the Final Word**: End immediately on the punchline callback. Zero outro, zero *"subscribe for more"*.

### B. 2D-in-3D Look & Shader Laws (`cartoon-3d-short` & `cartoon-audio-short`)
6. **Plum Outlines (`#241726`), Never Pure Black on Characters**: Character cards use `600×1000` Canvas2D textures (`soles at y = 975`), superellipse bean heads (`H/W = 0.94`, `nTop = 1.75`, `nBot = 2.30`), and variable-width `#241726` ink (`3:1` thick/thin weighted to `64°` bottom-right).
7. **3.5 Head-Heights Adult Proportion**: Head `1.0 HH`, torso bean `1.1 HH`, single-stroke `5.8u` stick legs `1.4 HH`, `5.5u` ink-dot hands. Default eye is `lidded` half-disc (`0.26 W` spacing) — **no blush ovals, no specular eye catchlights**.
8. **Screen-Space `InkPass` Runs BEFORE `LensBlurEffect`**: World outlines (`ink.ts`) must be a standalone `Pass` (never merged with bloom/grade) placed **before** DOF so background outlines blur softly with the lens.
9. **`NOINK = 3` Layer Mask Is Mandatory**: Every 2D character billboard, sky dome, cloud card, smoke puff, and water sheet MUST set `enableNoInk(node)` (`layers.enable(3)`) so the depth-edge shader never draws a rectangular box around transparent cards.
10. **Two-Tone `shade()` Material + Backlit Golden Hour**: Never use raw default PBR. Inject `shade()` via `onBeforeCompile` to capture shadow-map visibility (`sunVis`) and mix explicit `uShadeCol` and `uLitCol`. In golden-hour shots, place the sun ahead (`az 6–15°, el 7–9°`) and shift shadows toward **violet-blue** (`B/R ratio 1.35–3.0x` lit).
11. **Card Shade Floor (`V ≥ 0.86`) + Ground Contact Shadow Blobs**: Never use `emissiveMap` on character cards (it triggers bloom glow). Use `cardShade()` with a brightness floor of `0.86` and place a multiply-blended soft radial contact blob (`0.7 × 1.1m`) on the ground under every character.

### C. Camera, Motion & Acting Laws
12. **No Camera Hold Is Ever Static (`≥4f`)**: Every hold must creep via `push(cam, amt)` at `0.15–0.35%/frame` (`0.6%/f` on punch words). Frozen frames (`diff < 0.25` for `≥12f`) fail `short_qa.py`.
13. **Hard Cuts on Big Jumps; Smootherstep Glides (`18–40f`) Within a Beat**: Never cut between two cameras `<1.5m` and `<15°` apart (causes a jump-cut pop). Keep glide velocity `≤25 px/frame` at 720p.
14. **7-Frame Sign-Flipping Impact `KICKS`**: Impacts use deterministic alternating-sign decay (`1 - u`) over `7 frames` (`dy` + roll `da`), never random per-frame noise.
15. **2-Frame Reaction Lead & 4-Frame Blinks**: Expression changes (`mouth='o'`, `brows='v'`) start **2 frames before** the spoken punch word. Characters blink every `0.5–2.3s` (`4 frames`: `0.5, 1, 1, 0.5`) and across every expression swap.

### D. Low-Poly 3D (`make-3d-video`) Laws
16. **Every `shots.js` Scene Must Move Both Subject AND Camera**: Never hold a static low-poly mesh. Combine `KAZ.orbit`, `KAZ.dolly`, `KAZ.pan`, or `KAZ.flyThrough` with continuous object rotation/bobbing and staggered `KAZ.enter()` spring entrances.
17. **Escalating HUD Telemetry**: Pair the 3D scene with the `--u` scaled HUD overlay (`hud.tl` escalating value, `hud.tr` status pill, `hud.cap` 2-line serif italic narration, and `hud.cards` glass metrics).

### E. Readability & Anti-Slop Geometry Laws
18. **The Swing / Hanging Attachment Law**: Any hanging prop (swing seat, lantern, pendulum, rope) must pivot around its **top attachment point (`y = anchorY`)** and terminate visibly on a branch/beam in-frame with two ropes straddling the character. Never rotate a hanging prop around its bottom center.
19. **Chest-Height Railings & `>2m` Foreground Clearance**: Bridge/balcony railings must sit at `y ≤ 0.9m` (chest height on a `2.15m` card) so faces and arms are never bisected by horizontal bars. Keep non-framing props `>2m` from the camera lens.
20. **Sign Faces Must Be `MeshBasicMaterial` + `NOINK`**: Never apply directional `shade()` to painted sign faces or text banners, and always verify historical flag geometry (e.g., `1880s` Britain = Union Jack, not St. George's Cross).

### F. Audio, SFX & Rendering Laws
21. **The 2–4 Frame SFX Lead-In Law**: Transition whooshes and risers must peak **2–4 frames BEFORE** the visual cut (`t_cut - 0.08s`), handing off to a low-end impact thud (`60–90 Hz`) on frame 0 of the new shot.
22. **`-14 LUFS` Integrated Loudness & `-6 dB` Gated Mix**: Normalize narration to `-14 LUFS` (`TP = -1.0 dBTP`). Mix music + SFX at **-6 dB gated RMS relative to the voice** (`0.28×` linear gain when voice is active, ducking `35%` under speech).
23. **Always Map Audio Explicitly & Never Re-Render Video on Audio-Only Changes**: When merging rendered video and mixed audio, always run `ffmpeg -y -i video.mp4 -i mix.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -shortest final.mp4` (`render.mjs` produces a silent video stream that masks input 1 unless `-map` is explicit). When the user requests audio or voice changes on an already-rendered video, **never re-render the video frames** — copy the existing video stream (`-c:v copy`) and swap in the new audio track with `-map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0`.
24. **Auto-Tiered Best-Available TTS System (Fish Audio S2.1-Pro → Kokoro-82M → Edge-TTS → Local OS)**: Unless the user explicitly requests a specific voice/engine, automatically select the **best available TTS model** in priority order (see `.agents/skills/fish-audio-tts/SKILL.md`): (1) **`fish-audio` MCP** (`s2.1-pro` or `$0` `s2.1-pro-free` with inline stage tags like `[whispers sweetly]`, `[laughing nervously]`, `[sigh]`, `[excited]`, `[pause]`, 83 languages, 2M+ voices, zero-shot 10–30s cloning, Text-to-Voice Design, and `transcribe-1-pro` STT), (2) **`kokoro-tts` MCP** (`agent-fm`, 82M-param local ONNX neural TTS), (3) **`edge-tts` MCP / CLI** (`en-AU-WilliamNeural`, `rate="+4%"`), (4) **`local-os-tts` MCP** (`text-to-speech-mcp`).
24. **Redirect `REMOTION_TMP` Before Multi-Render Loops**: Each Remotion render writes a `1.9 GB` webpack bundle to `$TMPDIR/remotion-webpack-bundle-*` and does not clean it up on crash. Redirect `TMPDIR` to a scratch folder and purge `remotion-*` before rendering.
25. **Never Ship Without `short_qa.py` + Post-Completion Visual Inspection (`[[Post-Completion Visual QA — What, How & Why to Fix]]`)**: On **every completed video** (all 5 tracks), run `python scripts/short_qa.py final.mp4 out/qa_visual` (`0` black frames, `0` unplanned white flashes, `0` frozen spans `≥12f`), extract readable Frame `0` + `1fps`/cut-midpoint stills (`540×960`+), and **visually inspect** every shot for occlusion, 9:16 UI safe zones, grounding, `NOINK` boxes, lighting, acting, and captions—fixing and re-verifying before delivery.

Strip multi-line HUD boxes, bottom headlines, and extra screen text (`≥90%` of on-screen text must be subtitles). See `[[04 Next-Generation Retention, Subtitles & Audio Spec]]`.
