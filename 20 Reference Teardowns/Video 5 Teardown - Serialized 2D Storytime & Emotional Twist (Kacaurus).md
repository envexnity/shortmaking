## 3. Production Rules Borrowed from `Kacaurus`

1. **The Serialized Trilogy Loop (`Bagian 1/3 → 3/3`)**:
   - Splitting a 3.5-minute story into **3 tightly linked `70–90s` episodes** triples total watch-time per viewer and turns one idea into 3 algorithmic uploads that cross-feed each other via pinned comments and playlists.
2. **Plot via Physical Props (`Photo` → `Stopwatch/Whistle` → `Slanted Note with Heart`)**:
   - Every major plot turn is anchored to an **in-world prop insert close-up** (`DOF cInf = 14px` on the handwritten note), not spoken exposition.
3. **Environmental SFX as a Narrative Turning Point**:
   - Instead of only using SFX for UI transitions, make a **diegetic world sound (the roadside whistle at `0:40`)** the turning point of the scene—and put that sound image directly in the video title.
4. **Longer Breathing Room (`1.6–2.4 wps` + Emotional Music)**:
   - Unlike `3.7 wps` trivia shorts, personal anime/storytime shorts let comedic deadpan beats and romantic reactions breathe for `0.6–1.2s` over warm acoustic/lofi music swells.
