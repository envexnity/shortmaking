- **Format**: `1080×1920` (9:16 Vertical Short, rendered at `720×1280` EEVEE RT+GI `AgX - Punchy` and Lanczos-upscaled to `1080×1920` + crisp `8sb2R11FeVw` black-pill subtitles), `30 fps`

- **Target Duration**: `~35–40 seconds` (`136 words` across `16 beats`)

## Script

In this world, everyone is born with a hidden survival timer—and it resets every few seconds.

To stay alive, people have to constantly pull invisible gas from the atmosphere into their bodies using two internal pouches called lungs. They do this through holes in their face. If anyone stops doing it for even a few minutes, their screen goes black and they permanently log out of the server.

The craziest part? There is no pause button. You have to do it while walking, while eating, and even while completely unconscious in your sleep. Your body automatically forces you to keep pulling the gas in and pushing it back out, over and over, roughly 20,000 times a day.

Most players don't even realize they're doing it until someone mentions it.

Suddenly, the automatic mode turns off, and you have to press the button manually. Good luck.
