# Napoleon vs. 3,000 Rabbits (1807) — 3D Kaz-style Vertical Short

## Facts (sources: David Chandler "The Campaigns of Napoleon"; Thiébault Memoirs; Smithsonian / History.com)
- July 1807, following the Treaties of Tilsit (ending War of the Fourth Coalition with Russia and Prussia).
- Chief of Staff Marshal Louis-Alexandre Berthier organized a celebratory rabbit hunt for Napoleon Bonaparte and senior officers.
- Instead of trapping wild hares, Berthier's men collected hundreds to roughly 3,000 domesticated farm rabbits from local farmers.
- When the cages opened, the tame rabbits did not flee; associating humans in coats with being fed, they swarmed Napoleon directly.
- Napoleon initially laughed and swatted with his riding crop before the rabbits flanked him, climbed his coat, and drove him back to his imperial carriage.
- Even as the coachman drove off, rabbits chased and jumped into the carriage before it escaped at a gallop.

## Beats (voice: en-AU-WilliamNeural, +4%, steady deadpan tone)
1. In 1807, Napoleon Bonaparte went to battle against three thousand rabbits.
2. And lost.
3. He had just defeated Russia and Prussia, so his generals threw a celebratory hunt.
4. His chief of staff, Berthier, packed three thousand cages onto a sunny French field.
5. When the cages opened, the rabbits didn't run away.
6. They formed a solid wall of fur and charged straight at Napoleon.
7. Luckily, the Emperor laughed and swatted them back with his riding crop.
8. Unluckily, there were three thousand of them, and they flanked his boots.
9. You see, Berthier hadn't caught wild hares, he bought tame farm rabbits.
10. To them, a man in a coat wasn't a hunter, he was the waiter bringing cabbage.
11. Soon they were climbing his jacket, chewing his buttons, and swarming his generals.
12. Napoleon sprinted backward to his royal carriage and slammed the door.
13. The rabbits chased the wheels down the road and leapt through the windows.
14. The French army retreated. The rabbits stayed.
15. And the man who conquered Europe was defeated by dinner time.
