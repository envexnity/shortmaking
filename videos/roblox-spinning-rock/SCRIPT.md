## Script

In this world, everyone lives on a giant rock spinning at a thousand miles an hour.

Nobody feels it, and there are no guardrails. The only thing stopping you from flying off the map is an invisible force gluing your shoes to the dirt.

And that rock isn't even parked. It's whipping around an even bigger burning rock at sixty-seven thousand miles an hour—while the whole system flies through an infinitely expanding empty void.

You see, it's like standing on a merry-go-round strapped to a rollercoaster inside a pitch-black lobby that never stops stretching.

Yet everyone on the rock is just casually walking their dog, paying taxes, and arguing about traffic—completely ignoring the fact that if the rock ever hits the brakes for one second, everyone gets launched sideways into space.
