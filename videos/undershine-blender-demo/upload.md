## Description
Every stone bridge in this valley was built facing the wrong way—until sunset hits the river and the shadows line up with the ancient shrine beneath the cliff.

Built 100% in Blender 4.2 (EEVEE Next) using our Track 7 Undershine 3D-to-Pixel-Art pipeline: native 270×480 integer-scaled pixels, hue-shifted shadows, Ghibli spherical foliage normals, screen-space normal/depth outlines, and 8 cinematic multi-angle cameras.
