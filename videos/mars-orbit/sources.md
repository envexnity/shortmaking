## 1. Quantitative Claims Verified Against NASA / JPL / ESA

| Claim in Video | Measured Figure | Human Comparison Used | Primary Source | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Surface atmospheric pressure | `~6 mbar` (`~6.1 hPa` mean; `~600 Pa`) vs Earth sea-level `1,013.25 mbar` | Under `1%` (`0.6%`) of Earth's air pressure | NASA Mars Fact Sheet (NSSDC / GSFC) | Verified |
| Ebullism (body water boiling point) | At `~6.3 mbar` (Armstrong limit `~62.5 hPa`), water boils at `37°C` | Your exact internal body temperature (`37°C` / `98.6°F`) | NASA Bioastronautics Data Book / Armstrong Limit | Verified |
| Martian surface temperatures | Average `-63°C` (`~ -60°C`); polar winter nights drop below `-125°C` to `-140°C` | Colder than the coldest night ever recorded in Antarctica (`-89.2°C` Vostok) | NASA Mars Exploration Program / Mars Fact Sheet | Verified |
| Toxic soil perchlorates | `~0.5–1%` calcium/magnesium perchlorates in Phoenix soil wet chemistry (`2008`) | Toxic salts that disrupt human thyroid iodine uptake | NASA Phoenix Mars Lander / JPL / MECA (`2008–2009`) | Verified |
| Surface radiation dose | `~0.64 mSv/day` galactic cosmic rays + solar energetic particles (Gale Crater) | Equivalent to `~6 chest X-rays` (`~0.1 mSv` each) per day | NASA Curiosity / MSL RAD (`Hassler et al., Science 2013/2014`) | Verified |
| Opportunity rover lifespan | `90 sols` (`~92.5 Earth days`) nominal mission vs `14 years, 136 days` (`5,111 sols`, `2004–2018`) | Survived `~55×` longer than planned before planet-encircling dust storm (`2018`) | NASA/JPL Mars Exploration Rover Opportunity Mission | Verified |
| Earth-Mars light delay | Up to `~22.4 minutes` one-way at solar conjunction / aphelion (`~401 million km`) | Message takes `22 minutes` one-way (`~44 min` round-trip) | NASA JPL Horizons / Deep Space Network | Verified |
