# Sources & NASA/JPL Fact-Check — What Would Kill You First on Venus?

## Verified NASA / JPL Facts
- **Size & Gravity ("Earth's Twin")**: Venus radius `6,051.8 km` (`94.9%` of Earth), surface gravity `8.87 m/s²` (`90.5%` of Earth `9.80 m/s²`). *(NASA Venus Fact Sheet — NSSDC)*
- **Surface Pressure (`92 bar`)**: `92 bar` (`~92×` Earth sea-level `1.013 bar`), equivalent hydrostatic pressure to `~900 m` (`~3,000 ft`) below Earth's ocean surface. *(NASA Venus Fact Sheet / Pioneer Venus Multiprobe)*
- **Surface Temperature (`464–465°C`)**: Mean surface temperature `737 K` (`464°C` / `867°F`), exceeding the melting point of lead (`327.5°C`), despite orbiting at `0.723 AU` (nearly `2×` Mercury's `0.387 AU`). *(NASA Venus Fact Sheet)*
- **Sulfuric Acid Clouds & Virga**: Upper clouds (`48–70 km`) consist of concentrated sulfuric acid (`H₂SO₄`) droplets; precipitation evaporates mid-air due to intense lower-atmosphere heat before reaching the ground. *(NASA Mariner 10 & Pioneer Venus)*
- **Earth–Venus Radio Delay**: One-way light time varies from `2.2 minutes` (inferior conjunction, `~0.26 AU`) to `14.4 minutes` (superior conjunction, `~1.73 AU`) — up to ~15 minutes. *(JPL Horizons / NASA Solar System Exploration)*
- **50 km Cloud Deck Habitability**: At `~50–55 km` altitude, atmospheric pressure is `~1 bar` and temperature is `~20–30°C` ("room temperature"), though still surrounded by `CO₂` and sulfuric acid aerosols. *(NASA GISS / Geoffrey Landis)*

## Unproven / Open Questions
- **Active Volcanism Extent**: While Magellan radar revisits (*Herrick & Hensley 2023*) detected a deformed volcanic vent on Maat Mons, the total present-day eruption rate remains an active research question (not overstated on-screen).
