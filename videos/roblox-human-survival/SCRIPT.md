## Script (Exact User Script)

In this world, you have to constantly pull invisible gas into your chest through the holes in your face. You have to do this non-stop—while eating, traveling, and even sleeping—because if you stop for just three minutes, your lungs shut down and it’s an instant game over.

In this world, you have to slam pieces of skin over your eyeballs every few seconds. You have to do this to keep your eyes wet, otherwise your vision gets blurry and your eyes literally start burning.

In this world, you have to pour clear liquid down your throat multiple times a day. Your character is mostly made of this liquid, so if you don't keep refilling your tank, your internal organs start failing.

In this world, you have to crush up dead plants and animals using tiny rocks in your mouth. You have to do this so your stomach acid can melt them down and turn them into fuel just so you can keep walking around.

In this world, you have to lie down in a dark room, go completely unconscious, and hallucinate for eight hours straight every single day. If you skip this, your brain stops saving data, your stamina bar hits zero, and your character starts breaking down.
